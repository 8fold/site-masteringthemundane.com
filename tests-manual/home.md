# Home domain review

Review `/` on the verified worktree preview.

- The shared header has one h1. Four h2 containers appear in order: Time,
  People, Space, Money. Menu has no domain shortcuts; its domain links in the
  page body still reach their corresponding sections.
- Each domain has clear, calm copy and links to Coaching and Services. Check
  that the wording represents the actual offerings, particularly People:
  its Services link explains availability rather than promising a new service.
- Home alone is current in Menu. Resources and Books are also available in
  Menu, and "Apps" links to the apps site. Test native popover
  keyboard operation, dismissal, focus return and skip-to-main. The text-only
  Menu button underline uses 2px at rest and 3px on hover.
- Check 320px, 900px, zoom, light/dark and forced colors. Confirm readable
  content spacing, no overflow and heading navigation with assistive technology.
- Follow Coaching and Services without submitting a booking. No planned app
  features are advertised.
