We work together to update and refine the systems you use to manage your daily life.

Our work focuses on four practical domains: **time, people, space, and money**. We can also explore other specific points of friction as needed.

One of our primary goals is to help you build reliable, low-demand structures so that you eventually become independent of this practice, rather than reliant on ongoing guidance.

## Before we begin

We start with a free **30-minute introductory call**. This call gives us space to discuss where you are feeling stuck, clarify how we can help, and decide if working together is the right fit. There is no pressure or obligation to sign up.

[Schedule a 30-minute introduction (No cost)](https://cal.com/mastering-the-mundane/introduction)

## How sessions work

All coaching sessions are 60 minutes long. You can choose between two flexible ways to work together:

* **Ad-hoc sessions ($100 [.United States Dollars](USD) / session):** Best for addressing a single, specific point of friction or setting up a target system.
* **Membership ($50 USD / month):** Includes one 60-minute session per billing period. Any additional sessions scheduled within that billing period are billed at the standard ad-hoc rate.

Sessions take place via video call (a link is sent automatically upon booking), phone, or in person (travel fees billed separately if required).
